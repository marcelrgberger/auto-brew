## 1. WHY THIS DOCUMENT EXISTS

Several documents govern the relationship, and they occasionally address the same subject. Until now
three of them each claimed precedence in a different order. Under § 305c(2) BGB and Art. 5 of Directive
93/13/EEC, an ambiguity of that kind is resolved against the party that drafted it — so the
contradiction did not create flexibility, it created a rule chosen by whoever read it last.

This document states the order once. Every other document defers to it.

## 2. ORDER OF PRECEDENCE

Where two documents address the same subject and cannot be read together, the higher entry prevails:

1. **Mandatory law of your place of habitual residence.** Nothing below can reduce a right that the law of your country, state, province or territory gives you and does not allow to be waived.
2. **The store's terms**, for everything about the purchase itself, **where the store is the seller of your purchase**. AutoBrew is not sold through a store and is free of charge, so this entry has no application to it — see § 3.
3. **Product-specific terms:** the EULA and, for the source code, the MIT License under which it is published. Where the EULA and the MIT License conflict, the MIT License prevails for the code it covers.
4. **The Terms of Service**, for everything the product-specific terms do not address.
5. **Ancillary policies:** the Support and Update Policy, the Coordinated Vulnerability Disclosure Policy and the Trademark disclaimer.

The Privacy Policy sits outside this order. It describes processing rather than granting or limiting
contractual rights, and it is never overridden by a contractual document.

## 4. WHICH DOCUMENTS APPLY TO WHICH PRODUCT

Not all of them apply to every app. A document is shipped with an app only where its subject matter
exists in that app — an Acceptable Use Policy has nothing to govern where there is no shared resource,
and a copyright takedown procedure has nothing to act on where no content is hosted for users.

AutoBrew ships the following documents, all in Settings → Legal: Privacy Policy, California Notice at Collection, Terms of Use, EULA, Legal Framework Overview, Support and Update Policy, Security Information, Vulnerability Disclosure, Imprint, Trademark and Open-Source Licenses. AutoBrew has no purchases, subscriptions, accounts, AI features, health data or shared resources, so the documents for those subjects are absent because they do not apply, not because they were forgotten.
