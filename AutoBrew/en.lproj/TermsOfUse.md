## 2. SERVICES

### 2.1 Description

AutoBrew is a macOS menu-bar application that keeps Homebrew packages up to date according to the update policy you set, provides a graphical interface to browse, install, upgrade and remove Homebrew casks, and creates, restores and migrates snapshots of applications' user data on your Mac. It is:

- **Open source** under the MIT License — source code at https://github.com/marcelrgberger/auto-brew
- **Free of charge** — no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, no paid tier
- **Local** — it runs on your Mac and requires no account and no backend service of the Provider

AutoBrew requires a working Homebrew installation and runs the `brew` command on your Mac.
