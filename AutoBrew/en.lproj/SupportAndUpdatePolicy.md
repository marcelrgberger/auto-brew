# SUPPORT AND UPDATE POLICY

## How long the software is maintained, and what that promise means

**Application:** AutoBrew

## 1. THE COMMITMENT

**The support period for each version of AutoBrew is at least five years from the day that version is placed on the market** — for AutoBrew, the day the version is published on GitHub Releases. The period is stated with month and year in AutoBrew's Security Information (Settings → Legal → Security Information).

**That floor is the whole commitment.** Whether an app remains available, gains features, or is
withdrawn is a business decision this Policy does not promise anything about — § 4 describes what
happens on withdrawal. What the five years guarantee is that a version you acquired keeps receiving
the security and conformity updates it needs, for the stated period, whether or not the app is still
offered.

## 2. WHY THIS DOCUMENT EXISTS

Two separate obligations meet here, and they are usually confused with each other.

**§ 327f BGB and Art. 8(2) of Directive (EU) 2019/770** require the trader to provide updates —
including security updates — that are necessary to keep a digital product in conformity, for the period
the consumer may reasonably expect. For a continuously supplied product such as a subscription, that is
the whole contractual term. This duty cannot be excluded: Art. 22 of the Directive makes it mandatory.

**Art. 13(8) of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 (Cyber Resilience Act)** will require, from 11 December 2027,
that a support period be determined and published, and that it reflect how long the product is
reasonably expected to be in use.

**They are not the same thing, and conflating them is the mistake this section exists to avoid.**
§ 327f is a duty measured against the consumer's reasonable expectation — open-ended, assessed after the
fact, and for a subscription it runs for the whole contractual term. Art. 13(8) CRA requires a
**determined and published** period, known at the point of sale, expressed as a concrete span.

A formula such as "as long as the app is offered, plus two years" satisfies the first and fails the
second: at the moment of purchase it yields no end date. Hence the fixed five-year floor in § 1 — a
determined, published span, known at purchase, and deliberately not padded with open-ended promises
that a one-person company cannot guarantee to keep.

## 3. WHAT AN UPDATE COVERS

| Included | Not included |
|---|---|
| Security fixes | New features |
| Fixes required to keep the app working on supported OS versions | Support for OS versions the platform vendor has itself dropped |
| Fixes required to keep the described functionality available | Changes to the described functionality |
| Changes required by law | Changes requested by an individual user |

Updates are delivered as new releases on GitHub Releases, through AutoBrew's built-in updater (Sparkle) and through the Homebrew tap.

## 4. WITHDRAWAL OF AN APP

An app may be withdrawn **at any time** — continued availability is not promised anywhere
in these documents. What is regulated is how, and what survives:

- The withdrawal is announced in the app and in the release notes, **at least 30 days in advance**, stating the date and the end of the support period that follows from it.
- **Security and conformity updates continue** for versions already acquired, until the support period stated for that version ends (§ 1). Withdrawal does not shorten it.
- **Subscriptions:** AutoBrew has none, so no prepaid period can be affected.
- **Apps that work locally keep working.** Withdrawal ends the distribution of new downloads; it does not reach into your device.

An app withdrawn without such an announcement continues to be supported. The clock does not
run on a decision the user was never told about.

## 5. RIGHT TO CHANGE THE SERVICE

The service may be changed only where there is a valid reason, which is limited to:

- adapting to a change in law, case law or a platform policy
- closing a security vulnerability
- maintaining compatibility with an operating system or a platform service
- replacing a third-party service that is discontinued by its provider

Any such change is announced 30 days in advance where it is not required to be immediate for security
reasons. Where a change materially disadvantages you, you may stop using AutoBrew and keep using the version you have, which remains supported for its stated period (§ 1).

Discontinuation of the service as a whole is not a change under this section; it is the withdrawal
under § 4.

## 6. WHAT THIS DOES NOT AFFECT

The statutory rights for digital products under §§ 327 ff. BGB and the national implementations of
Directive (EU) 2019/770 apply in full and are not limited by anything above.
